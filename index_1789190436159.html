Defense Milestones & Heritage Timeline

Chronicles of valor, strategic resilience, and historic operations.

Operational Theater:
November 1947

Battle of Badgam

A pivotal defensive stand in the Kashmir Valley where Major Somnath Sharma and the 4th Kumaon Regiment held off numerically superior forces, securing the critical Srinagar airfield.

Ground Defense
November 18, 1962

Battle of Rezang La

Charlie Company of 13 Kumaon under Major Shaitan Singh defended the Chushul Valley at 16,000 feet in Ladakh. In freezing blizzards, 114 soldiers fought to the last round, repelling multiple heavy frontal assaults.

High Altitude Last Stand
September 1965

Battle of Asal Uttar

One of the largest armored tank battles since World War II. Defenders tactically breached canal bunds to bog down enemy Patton tanks in slush, neutralizing over 90 armored vehicles.

Armored Warfare
December 4, 1971

Operation Trident

A daring nighttime naval strike employing anti-ship missile boats that penetrated defended harbor waters, showcasing forward naval deterrence and strategic precision.

Naval Strike
December 5, 1971

Battle of Longewala

A legendary defense where 120 soldiers of 23 Punjab with a single anti-tank weapon held an isolated Thar desert outpost through the night against 40+ tanks until air support arrived at dawn.

Infantry & Air Defense
December 15–17, 1971

Battle of Basantar

Critical armored engagement in the Shakargarh sector. Second Lieutenant Arun Khetarpal in his Centurion tank repulsed continuous armored counter-attacks, destroying multiple tanks despite lethal incoming fire.

Armored Breakthrough
April 1984

Operation Meghdoot

The world's highest tactical deployment atop the Siachen Glacier at altitudes exceeding 18,000 feet, securing all major passes along the Saltoro Ridge in sub-zero glaciated terrain.

High Altitude Operation
November 3, 1988

Operation Cactus

Swift expeditionary intervention in the Maldives launched within hours. Paratroopers of the 50th Independent Parachute Brigade secured critical airfields, thwarting a hostile mercenary coup.

Airborne Intervention
May 1999

Operation Safed Sagar

Groundbreaking deployment of combat air power at extreme Himalayan altitudes during the Kargil Conflict, employing precision laser-guided munitions against fortified high-altitude positions.

High Altitude Air Strike
July 1999

Battle of Tiger Hill

A decisive combined-arms high-altitude assault during the Kargil Conflict, featuring precision artillery fire and vertical rock-face climbs to reclaim dominant heights overlooking National Highway 1D.

Mountain Warfare
April 2015

Operation Raahat

A massive tri-service evacuation mission from war-torn Yemen. Indian Navy vessels and Air Force C-17 Globemasters safely evacuated over 5,600 people, including nearly 1,000 foreign citizens from 41 nations.

Tri-Service Evacuation
January 2020

Integrated Theater Commands & CDS

The institutional creation of the Chief of Defence Staff (CDS) and Department of Military Affairs, inaugurating unified tri-service theaterization and modern integrated defense capabilities.

Tri-Service Modernization

The Battlefield Is More Than the Battle

Terrain, logistics, coordination, and resolve decide whether a defensive line holds.

01
TERRAIN AS A WEAPON

Read the ground before you read the map.

Desert visibility, mountain altitude, river crossings, and narrow passes can turn a smaller force into a disciplined defensive system.

02
COMBINED ARMS

Infantry holds. Armour breaks. Artillery shapes.

The strongest operations synchronize soldiers, tanks, engineers, fire support, and air power instead of treating them as separate weapons.

03
LOGISTICS UNDER FIRE

Every frontline depends on a supply line.

Fuel, ammunition, medical evacuation, communications, and cold-weather sustainment are often the hidden difference between endurance and collapse.

04
JOINT RESPONSE

Modern defence is a network, not a single service.

Air, land, maritime, cyber, space, and humanitarian capabilities now converge into one operating picture and one chain of decision.

OPERATIONAL PRINCIPLE: Hold the objective. Protect the force. Adapt faster than the threat.
